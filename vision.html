Vision Pathway

Light entering the eye is converted into electrical signals by the retina, relayed through the optic nerve and thalamus, and finally interpreted by the visual cortex at the back of the brain.

1 Light hits the retina
2 Retina converts light → electrical signal
3 Signal travels the optic nerve
4 Relayed through the thalamus
5 Visual cortex interprets the image
Why Two Nerves Cross

At the optic chiasm, signals from the inner half of each retina cross to the opposite side of the brain — which is part of why each hemisphere handles one half of your visual field.
