Reflex Arc

A spinal reflex bypasses the brain entirely — the signal loops through the spinal cord and back out to muscle, which is why you pull your hand away from something hot before you consciously "feel" it.

1 Skin receptor detects heat
2 Sensory neuron fires toward spinal cord
3 Spinal cord relays signal (no brain needed)
4 Motor neuron fires toward muscle
5 Muscle contracts — hand withdraws
Why It's Fast

Because the loop stays entirely within the spinal cord, a reflex can complete in well under half a second — faster than routing the decision through the brain and back.
