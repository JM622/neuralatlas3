Memory Formation

Click Rehearse repeatedly to reactivate the connection between two neurons. Repeated, closely-timed activation triggers long-term potentiation (LTP) — the synapse physically strengthens. Stop rehearsing early and watch it fade.

Why It Fades

Short-term traces decay if not reinforced — this is modeled here as a slow passive decay. Once potentiation crosses the consolidation threshold, the connection is treated as stable, mirroring how long-term memories resist forgetting far better than fresh, unrehearsed ones.
